# Ultimate Reality

The work proposes a logic of “ultimate reality” with a self-referential algebraic structure — operators I and D, where ID=1 and I+D=0 — and develops a model of consciousness, time (quadruplication of the present moment), and actual infinity. The final sections outline an architecture for a Self-Referential AI based on these principles.

Here is the structure of the PDF:

1. I-Reflection. Ground of knowledge. Strict definition.
2. Consciousness as reality through relation as existence.
3. Dialectics of consciousness. Reciprocal  categories.
4. Self-awareness as the Time. 
Time structure: quadruple of the present moment.
5. Actual infinity. Superposition of infinities.
6. Self-measurement of actual infinity. 
Reciprocal operators of identity I and difference D. 
ID=1, I+D=0, 1=0. Reality as a broken  symmetry. 
Quaternions, projective plane, numbers, fractals. 
Foundation of physics.
7. Space of the present moment.
8. Uncertainty principle and measures of time. 
The Past and the Future.
9. Information, intellect, life. 
Negative feedback. Polchinski’s tachyon crystal. 
10. Otherworldly personality.
11. Goals and means.
12. A Self-Referential AI. 
Logic of consciousness and topological architecture.
13. Reality hacking. Tachionic AI.
